## About Me

</div>

> _"Not all those who wander are lost."_

Hi! I'm **Lincoln G. Santos**, aka **darkanum**. I've been building things with technology since **2003**: Ragnarok Online servers, training simulators, mobile games, payment rails for a digital bank, and now AI agents that review, fix and watch production code on their own. 🧭

Today I'm a **Founding AI Engineer @ [CoGrader](https://cograder.com)** and the founder of **[VapuLab](https://vapulab.com)**, a software lab that builds B2B products.

### Highlights

- **📈 2M → 15M+ customers** at Neon while I led the payments tribe tech (Boleto, Pix Cobrança, card acquiring, CDB)
- **⚡ Pix from day one** (Nov 2020) to Pix Cobrança for businesses, on a platform moving **US$1B+/month**
- **🧾 1M+ downloads and 700k+ active users** at MEI Fácil, from day one to the acquisition by Neon
- **🏆 Microsoft Imagine Cup 2013 winner in Brazil** with InsuOnline, a diabetes training game for doctors
- **🎮 160k+ downloads** on the games I shipped with my own studio, Red Stomp
- **🍎 100k+ teachers at 16k+ schools** use the AI grading I help build at CoGrader

## Experience

</div>

### 🤖 Founding AI Engineer @ CoGrader

AI grading for teachers, trusted by **100,000+ teachers** at **16,000+ schools across all 50 US states**, with **2.5M+ essays graded**.

- ✍️ Build the AI grading pipeline: multi-file submissions, grammar feedback and exports back to Google Classroom
- 🦾 Created the AI agents that run engineering on autopilot: one reviews every PR, one turns bug reports into fix PRs, and one patrols production and sends each issue to the engineer who knows the code best
- ☁️ Designed the infrastructure those agents run on, on Azure

### 🧪 Founder @ VapuLab

- 🧾 Built and runs **NotaFlow**, the lab's first commercial product, and the open-source **malphas** design system

### 🛡️ Tech Lead @ Too Seguros (BTG Pactual)

- 💳 Channels squad and the payments engine that serves every interface of the company

### 🏦 Payments Tech Coordinator @ Neon

- 📈 Led the payments tribe tech while Neon grew **from 2M to 15M+ customers**: Boleto, Pix Cobrança, card acquiring and CDB
- ⚡ On Pix from launch day, then took it from person-to-person to business collection with dynamic QR codes
- 🏛️ Built the platform for Neon's own-issued CDB (daily liquidity, up to 113% of CDI) after the Biorc acquisition

### 🧾 Staff Engineer @ MEI Fácil

- 🚀 Joined on day one and helped build the app that lets anyone open and run a MEI from their phone: CNPJ, invoices, tax guides and boletos
- 📈 **1M+ downloads and 700k+ active users** in under two years, until the acquisition by Neon in 2019

### 🎮 Founder, CTO & Sound Designer @ Red Stomp Studio

- 🥷 **Elemental Ninja**: 100k+ downloads on Google Play
- 🐟 **Farting Fishes**: 60k+ downloads

### 🚜 Game Developer @ Oníria

- 🏆 **InsuOnline**: a game that trains doctors to diagnose and treat diabetes. **Microsoft Imagine Cup 2013 winner in Brazil** (World Citizenship) and world finalist in St. Petersburg, later proven effective in a [randomized controlled trial](https://www.jmir.org/2017/3/e72)
- 🕹️ Training simulators for **Stara**, **Case** and **New Holland**, plus a lot of minigames
